# Community expectations

Keep this project welcoming, constructive, and focused on the work. Treat contributors and users with respect, including when discussing mistakes, differing technical opinions, or personal productivity challenges.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, impersonation, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. Do not use issues or discussions for spam or unsolicited promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate to the conduct and protect the people involved. Report harmful content using GitHub's reporting tools; do not repost private information to document it.
