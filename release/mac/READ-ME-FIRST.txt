JustThirty for Mac
A little access. A clear stopping point.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel.
Drag JustThirty to Applications, then open it. Use Set up background blocking
and approve the local service in System Settings if asked.

Review additions carefully: the app has no remove or reset control.
All selected apps and websites share 30 minutes a day on this Mac.
Start to unlock, pause to re-block and preserve unused time, resume later.
The timer keeps running while the app is closed or the Mac is asleep.

SAVE WORK IN BLOCKED APPS before pausing or ending access. Only explicitly
saved apps close; browsers stay open unless you blocked the browser app itself.
Host rules cover explicit domains. Proxies, alternate DNS behavior, existing
connections, or additional domains may bypass them. This is a personal
commitment tool; a Mac administrator retains control of the system.

Privacy: selections and timers stay on your Mac. No account, ads, or analytics.
