Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · JustThirty 1.3

JustThirty is a free focus app made by Farzam Hejazi. This policy covers the iPhone app, the Mac app, and this website.

What stays on your device

The apps store your block list and daily allowance locally. On iPhone, selections made through Apple’s Family Activity Picker use Apple’s privacy-preserving tokens. Domains you type are stored as domain names. The app and its monitoring extension share local state to restore blocking when an access session ends. The Mac app stores its enforcement state locally in a protected system folder.

The apps do not send us your selected apps, blocked domains, browsing history, timer usage, or other device activity. They include no analytics SDK, advertising, account, cloud sync, or tracking. Website suggestions are searched locally.

Apple permissions and system services

The iPhone app requests Screen Time authorization for individual device management. Apple controls that authorization and the Screen Time framework. The Mac app requires administrator approval for a local background service that updates explicit host rules and closes selected applications. These permissions serve the app’s focus controls.

Your controls

There is no in-app removal or reset for saved block-list entries. iOS lets you revoke Screen Time authorization or delete the app. Local data may be included in backups managed by your operating system. Removing software, backup restoration, or system administration may affect stored data and restrictions. JustThirty does not offer an online account or maintain a remote copy of your list.

This website

This website has no analytics or advertising scripts and does not set application cookies or save your timer preview. The preview runs only in your browser and does not control any app or website restrictions.

Vercel hosts the website and processes ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. See Vercel’s privacy policy. Apple and GitHub have their own policies when you follow links to their services.

Support

If you choose to contact us through GitHub, information you post may be public and is processed by GitHub. Do not post your private block list, contact details, credentials, or personal activity. You can ask general questions without supplying this information.

Changes and contact

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. Privacy questions can be raised through the project’s support page.
