04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few honest
answers.

Is it really 30 minutes across everything?

Yes. All selected apps and websites share one combined allowance on each device. Your iPhone and Mac have separate 30-minute allowances. This is a timer you start and pause, rather than a measurement of active browsing.

When does the allowance reset?

At midnight in the time zone where the app’s allowance was first created. Unused minutes don’t carry over. The app keeps that original time zone so a time-zone change doesn’t grant another day.

Can I remove something I’ve blocked?

There is no removal or reset control inside JustThirty. Review additions carefully. The device owner retains operating-system controls, including deleting the iPhone app or administering the Mac. JustThirty is a commitment tool, not a tamper-proof lock.

Will every website be blocked in every browser?

Coverage varies. iPhone filtering uses Apple’s Screen Time capabilities and browser support. The Mac app uses local host rules for explicit domains; proxies, alternative DNS behavior, and existing connections may bypass them. It isn’t a network firewall or universal content filter.

What does it cost?

JustThirty for Mac is free, with no subscription or in-app purchases. The iPhone release is also planned to be free. The source code is available publicly.
